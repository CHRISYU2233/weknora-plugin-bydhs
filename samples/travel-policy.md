# Travel Policy (sample)

> Sample document for testing WeKnora_plugin_byDHS. Everything here is fictional.

## Approval

Any trip that costs more than 5,000 CNY needs written approval from a department
manager before booking. International travel always needs approval.

## Booking

Flights and hotels are booked through the travel desk. Booking a hotel directly is
allowed only when the travel desk cannot serve the destination.

## Per diem

| Region | Per diem (CNY/day) |
| --- | --- |
| Domestic | 300 |
| International | 800 |

The per diem covers meals and local transport. Hotel and flights are reimbursed
separately against receipts.

## Reimbursement

Expense claims are submitted within 30 days of the trip. Claims submitted after
60 days are rejected unless a manager explains the delay in writing.
