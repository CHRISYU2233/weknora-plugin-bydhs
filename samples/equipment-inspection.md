# Equipment Inspection Handbook (sample)

> Sample document for testing WeKnora_plugin_byDHS. Everything here is fictional.

## Scope

This handbook covers the fictional devices **A-17**, **B-23** and **C-08** used in the
demo environment. It does not describe any real machine, line or site.

## Inspection cycle

| Device | Cycle | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A-17 | 36 hours | Equipment team |
| B-23 | 72 hours | Equipment team |
| C-08 | 48 hours | Maintenance team |

The inspection cycle for device A-17 is 36 hours. A missed cycle is an anomaly and must
be reported within 24 hours.

## What an inspection covers

1. Check that the safety cover is closed.
2. Record the pressure gauge reading; the normal range is 4.0 to 6.0.
3. Confirm there is no visible leakage.
4. Sign the inspection log.

## Records

Inspection records are archived before the 5th of each month. Records are kept for
three years.
